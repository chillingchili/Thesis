# Product context

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
This is the Android proof of concept for the Pascua–Leones pickleball serve thesis. Players review serve subtype, weight shift, paddle orientation and corrective feedback; researchers evaluate those outputs against independent coaching judgments. The thesis evaluates recorded clips and the existing Android demo also has live capture.

## Voice
Use short, concrete coaching instructions derived from Coach B's comments. Distinguish a measured result, missing data, and unvalidated research feedback. No consumer brand personality is specified in the thesis.

## Scope and references
Follow the existing Android results overlay and the thesis output states in section 4.5.3. Full navigation and consumer product design are outside the thesis scope. No external brand references or anti-references were supplied.

## Design principles
- Preserve independently available results when another stream fails.
- Withhold coaching when GRU confidence is below 0.60.
- State only what the available measurements support.
- Keep the result readable while the camera remains visible.
- Display feedback after the serve and hold it until the next capture.

## Accessibility
Use readable Android text sizing, allow long results to scroll, and express state in words as well as color. No formal accessibility certification target is specified by the thesis.
